Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Are these tools really 100% free?
Yes! Every single tool on Student Utility Hub is completely free to use without any hidden subscriptions, paywalls, or feature limits.
Do I need to create an account to use the calculators?
No account registration is required. We believe in frictionless access. Simply open the website and start using the tools immediately.
Are my uploaded files safe and private?
Absolutely. We utilize advanced client-side processing architecture. This means your images, audio files, and text are processed locally on your device's browser and are NEVER uploaded to our servers.
Can I use these tools on my mobile phone?
Yes, our entire platform is fully responsive and optimized for mobile devices, tablets, and desktop computers.
How accurate are the financial calculators?
Our EMI and SIP calculators use industry-standard mathematical formulas to ensure high precision for your financial planning. However, they should be used for estimation purposes.
What image formats does the Image Converter support?
Currently, our Image Converter supports rapid conversions between JPG, PNG, and modern WEBP formats.
Does the Audio Trimmer degrade audio quality?
No, the Audio Trimmer parses the audio data and exports the specific slice you selected into an uncompressed WAV format, preserving the original fidelity of that segment.
How does the JSON Formatter handle large files?
The JSON Formatter utilizes your browser's native JSON parsing engine, making it exceptionally fast even for massive JSON payloads.
Can I generate QR codes for Wi-Fi networks?
Yes, you can input standard Wi-Fi strings (e.g., WIFI:S:MyNetwork;T:WPA;P:MyPassword;;) into the QR Code Generator to create scannable Wi-Fi codes.
Why use client-side tools instead of server-side?
Client-side tools eliminate server upload times, reduce bandwidth usage, guarantee 100% data privacy, and are often significantly faster for the end-user.
